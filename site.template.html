Choose the right place

Loading, crashes, updates, graphics, controls or translations: open a post in the community’s #troubleshoot forum.

Card effects or rulings in an ocgcore duel: use the MyCard feedback forum. Reports in English are welcome.

Native-engine Solo: use #troubleshoot and name the engine.

For duel issues, attach the replay and identify the turn: .yrp3d for ocgcore, or the saved .mdreplay for native-engine Solo when available. If you cannot save a replay, explain why.
